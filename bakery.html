TWO KITCHENS, ONE FAMILY

The Exclusives 017 started in 2017 in Kwa-Thema, Springs. Today it has two divisions: Bakery & Confectionery, which starts baking in the early hours of every morning, and Eats, where kota orders open from around 11:00 am and the kitchen serves chicken, wings and burgers.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I pay?

Pay online after we confirm your order. We send you a QR code on WhatsApp: open your banking or payment app, scan it and pay. Then send us the payment confirmation. Only pay using a QR code or link that comes from our WhatsApp number.

How do I order?

Tap Add on the menu, open your order with the cart button, fill in your name and send it on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm. Your order can include bakery and Eats items together.

Do you make buckets for functions?

Yes. We do scones, sponge cakes and jam tarts in 5L, 10L and 20L buckets. Add one to your order, or message us with your date and number of guests.

Can I get packets of biscuits for an occasion?

Ask us on WhatsApp with the kind and amount you need, and we will quote you.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is available. The fee depends on where you are and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

When do you start baking?

We start in the early hours every morning. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm what is fresh today.
