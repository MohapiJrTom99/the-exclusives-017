REFUNDS & COMPLAINTS

All our food is made fresh to order, so we cannot take back food that was made correctly. We do want every order to be right.

  • If your order is wrong, missing items, or there is a problem with the food, message us on WhatsApp straight away.
  • Please send a photo of the food and your order details so we can sort it out quickly.
  • Where the mistake is ours, we will remake the item, replace what is missing or refund the affected item.
  • Refunds are paid back the same way you paid.
Message us on WhatsApp

DELIVERY

  • Delivery is available around Kwa-Thema and nearby parts of Springs.
  • The delivery fee depends on your address and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • We confirm an estimated time when we confirm your order. Busy times, weather and traffic can affect this.
  • Please be available at the address and keep your phone nearby so our driver can reach you.
  • You can also collect your order at 21514 Masibeni Str., Kwa-Thema, Springs.
  • For bakery buckets and large occasion orders, please order as early as you can and tell us the date you need them. We confirm availability and timing on WhatsApp.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

  • All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR) and may change without notice.
  • An order is accepted once we confirm it on WhatsApp. Items can sell out, and we will tell you if something is unavailable.
  • Payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp. Please only pay once you have our confirmation.
  • Photos on this site are examples. Real portions and presentation can differ slightly.
  • If you have allergies, tell us when you order. We cannot guarantee that any item is free of allergens.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of South Africa.

PRIVACY

We respect your privacy and handle your information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • We only collect what we need to fulfil your order: your name, delivery address and phone number.
  • Your order details are sent to us through WhatsApp. This site does not store them on a server.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.
  • You can ask us at any time to delete your details by emailing theexclusives017@gmail.com.
