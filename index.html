WELCOME TO THE EXCLUSIVES 017

Some places sell food. We create experiences.

Since 2017, The Exclusives 017 has lived by one belief: Good food is art — and what's art without creativity?

Now proudly located in the heart of Kwa-Thema, Springs, that belief still drives everything we bake, fry, stack, and serve today.

We are one kitchen, two divisions, one unforgettable taste:

EATS
This is where the streets come alive. Loaded kotas stacked high, golden Ama-fries, ice-cold drinks, and socials you don't want to miss. Kota orders open from around 11:00 am — come hungry.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I order?

Tap Add on the menu, open your order with the cart button, fill in your name and send it on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is now available. The delivery fee depends on where you are and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

What days and times are you open?

Monday to Saturday. Our bakery starts in the early hours, and kota orders open from around 11:00 am. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm today's times.

Do you have a bakery?

Yes. Our Bakery & Confectionery makes fresh bread, scones, sponge cakes, jam tarts and oat biscuits, plus buckets for functions.

What is a kota?

A kota is a hollowed-out loaf stacked with chips, egg, cheese, meat, fresh veg and sauces. Ours is built on egg-toasted bread.

Can I add extras?

Yes. Extra kota builds like bacon, Russian, rib burger, egg and cheese sauce are on the menu under Extra Kota Builds.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Message us on WhatsApp straight away with a photo. See our Refunds & Complaints policy.
